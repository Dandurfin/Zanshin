**In short:** the code is free. Only two picture files are mine. Change Zanshin
and share it as you like — just don't pretend your version is the official
Zanshin. This is here to protect the people who use it, not to make money.

## 1. What is free

All of the source code, under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 or later**
(see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). That includes the parts that make the look: the
ensō drawn in code, the colour themes, the layout.

The word *zanshin* is a general term from the martial arts. Nobody owns it,
and nothing here claims it.

## 2. What stays mine

Two artwork files:

- the app icon — `Dandurf.ico`;
- the dojo picture — `assets/images/dojo_noc.webp`.

You may share unmodified copies of the official Zanshin with them as they are.
A modified version has to use its own icon and picture, so it can't be
mistaken for the original.

Third-party files keep their own terms (for example, `sfx_click_reload.wav`
and `sfx_lock_ping.wav` in `assets/sounds/modern`: two sounds from Kenney's
*Interface Sounds* under the CC0 licence, which ship with the app).

## 4. Where the official Zanshin is

Only at **<https://github.com/Dandurfin/Zanshin>**: the source code, and a
ready-made ZIP on its Releases page, listed with its SHA-256 fingerprint so you
can check that you have the real file. There is no installer. A copy from
anywhere else is not from me.
