# Zanshin — the soul behind the app

*An ethos, not a spec. What Zanshin is, why it exists, and the principles it refuses to break.*

---

## The word

**Zanshin** (残心) is a term from the martial arts: the state of relaxed, total awareness — a calm, clear mind, ready for anything, that *remains* even after the strike lands. Not tension. Not absence. **Present readiness.**

That word is the whole design brief. Everything the app does is trying to give one thing back to the person at the keyboard: a moment of calm, aware presence in the middle of the action.

---

## Why it exists

We spend enormous parts of the day at the PC — playing, streaming, competing. And we slip. We fall into complete autopilot: either so absorbed that we lose the clock and the body entirely, or caught in needless tilt when things go wrong.

Zanshin is a subtle companion against both. It's meant to keep you from burning out, remind you to take a breath now and then, and keep you grounded. It isn't about complicated esoteric exercises. It's about **enjoying the game more, playing better, and not tilting over nothing.**

> *I grew up with games, like a lot of my generation. More goes on behind the eyes than anyone sees. Zanshin is my attempt to try it in peace: something that notices, wants nothing and sells nothing.*
> — the author

---

## What it is

A **local-first heart-rate companion for gamers.** It runs on your own machine. It listens to your heartbeat, shows a quiet heads-up readout, and offers gentle cues — *take a breath, unclench your jaw, feel the ground*. The voice waits for a **short pause in your input** — about 2.5 seconds with no key, mouse or controller — once your load has stopped climbing, and never while your pulse is in the Peak zone (at or above your own high heart-rate limit). The app can't see the game, so a still moment in the middle of a fight can count as a pause too. If no pause comes, at most a small silent picture appears, under the same rules.

The heart is the honest signal. The cue is just a door, delivered at the right moment.

## What it is deliberately **not**

- **Not a medical device.** Not a diagnosis, not a clinical number.
- **Not a fitness tester.** Its figures are rough, relative trends about *you* — not scores, not verdicts.
- **Not a nag.** It waits for a lull in your input, and never speaks while your pulse is in the Peak zone.
- **Not a wall** between you and the game. It touches nothing it doesn't have to.
- **Not a harvester.** Your data is yours. No telemetry, no analytics; your heart rate, sessions and settings stay on your machine — with one exception: the default online voice sends the wording of spoken cues to Microsoft to turn it into speech. That and the few other things that can touch the network are listed in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

Naming what it *isn't* is part of the honesty. A tool that overstates itself has already broken the first promise.

## The principles — the soul

**1. Presence over autopilot.**
The entire purpose is to return you to yourself, calmly, mid-play. Awareness is the product; the heart rate is only how it knocks.

**2. Calm alertness, not walls.**
Zanshin is neither clenched fear nor reckless abandon. It is relaxed, present readiness — the middle. The app models that: aware, ready, unhurried.

**3. Honest over polished.**
Ship it true, not perfect. Say out loud what it can't do. If a line of text promises more than the code delivers, the line is the bug — fix the words until they're true, and name plainly whatever can't be fixed yet. Truth without varnish.

**4. Open — freely inherited, freely passed on.**
The code is free and open under the GPLv3. Nothing this app does was invented here — the breathing, the focus, the unclenched jaw, the research behind it, the tools it's built with. It stands on the shoulders of giants who left their work in the open, and it stays open so the next person can read it, change it, and carry it on. What is passed on this way can never be closed. Only two pictures stay the author's own — the app icon and the dojo artwork (see LICENSE-DESIGN.md). Whoever carries the code on names their version as their own, never as the original.

**5. Whisper, don't shout.**
A companion, not a coach barking over your shoulder. Subtle by design. It shows up small, in the corner of the eye, and lets you keep playing.

**6. Know when to stop.**
*There is no prize for perfection — only an end to pursuing it.* The worth of the thing isn't in stars, or numbers, or who noticed. It's in the honest work made real, and in the few people it actually helps.

**7. Steward, don't capture.**
The idea is protected by keeping it open and unhurried — a seed grows on its own clock, not on anyone's gravity. Share the spark; never hand over the direction before it knows what it is.

## How the soul shows up in the build

- **The cue meets the pause.** The voice waits for a short lull in your input (~2.5 s) once the load has stopped climbing, and never in the Peak zone. The app can't see the game, so a quiet moment mid-fight can count too.
- **Strain is measured against your own calm.** The load number is read against *your* resting baseline, and errs toward under-reporting rather than crying wolf. It would rather be quiet than alarmist.
- **The app audits its own honesty.** It gets checked, deliberately, against a single question: *does it promise anything it can't deliver?* Gaps get written down and named, not buried.
- **Alpha is a beginning, not an apology.** The first public version is offered honest and rough on purpose — a real thing, out in the open, growing in daylight.

---

## For the one who reads between the lines

Between the input and the reaction, there is a gap. A held breath. A pause between rounds.

Most of the day we live as if that gap weren't there — reacting, chasing, tightening. Zanshin is a small, quiet attempt to hand it back to you: *the space to notice you have a body, a breath, and a choice, even in the middle of the fight.*

It doesn't ask you to be calm. It just leaves the door open, and trusts that whoever needs it will walk through.

Stay soft. Stay ready. Breathe.

**残**
